The settings page is opened from **Settings** in the sidebar and is divided into five sections: Profile, Security, Connected accounts, Downloads and Preferences.

## Profile

### Changing your user name

Change it in the user name field on the **Profile** page, up to 20 characters. While you type, the page tells you whether the user name is available. Click **Save** after changing it.

![The profile section of the settings](/assets/images/account-settings/en/personal@2x.webp)

### Changing your avatar, banner and bio

Your avatar, banner, bio and page background are changed on your profile. Open your profile and click **Edit profile**; after you choose a picture, a crop tool appears, and once the cropping is done, click **Save** to apply the change.

## Security

![The security section of the settings](/assets/images/account-settings/en/security@2x.webp)

### Changing your password

On the **Security** page, click **Change** in the password row and enter your current password and a new password. Requirements for the new password:

- At least 8 characters
- Contains an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, and a digit or symbol

After a successful change, you are signed out on your other devices and need to sign in again there. If you have forgotten your current password, click **Forgot password?** in the sign-in dialog and reset your password through the link in the email.

### Changing your email

Changing your email requires verifying both your current email and the new one:

1. Send a verification code to your current email and enter it
2. Enter the new email address, send a verification code to the new email and enter it, then confirm the change

### Passkeys

A passkey is a way to sign in without a password, using your device's biometrics (fingerprint, facial recognition) or device password for verification.

**Adding a passkey:**

1. Click **Add a passkey** and optionally enter a name (up to 128 characters) to make it easy to identify
2. Your browser shows a verification request; follow the prompts to complete the verification
3. After successful verification, the passkey is saved automatically and appears in the list

**Managing existing passkeys:**

Each passkey in the list shows its name, the time it was added and the time it was last used. Click the delete button to remove a passkey; the removal takes effect after you confirm it.

> If your browser or device does not support WebAuthn, this feature will not be available.

## Connected accounts

Manage the links between your account and other services:

- **Hikarinagi ID** — once linked, you can sign in with your Hikarinagi ID. While it is your only way to sign in, it cannot be unlinked; set a password or add a passkey first.
- **PotatoVN** — links your PotatoVN account. The password you enter is only used to request an access credential from PotatoVN and is not stored on this site; removing the link deletes none of your data on PotatoVN.

![The connected accounts section of the settings](/assets/images/account-settings/en/connections@2x.webp)

## Downloads

Choose the default download method and configure the targets of push downloads.

![The default download method and the game managers](/assets/images/account-settings/en/download@2x.webp)

Under **Game managers**, LunaBox, ReinaManager and PotatoVN can each be switched on or off; once switched on, they appear in the download menu.

The **aria2** part is for configuring the aria2 download client, so that game resources can be pushed to an aria2 running on this computer or on your local network:

| Field       | Description                                                            |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| RPC address | The JSON-RPC address of aria2, such as `http://127.0.0.1:6800/jsonrpc` |
| RPC secret  | The RPC secret of aria2 (if one is set)                                |
| Save to     | Leave empty to use aria2's default directory                           |

![The aria2 connection](/assets/images/account-settings/en/aria2@2x.webp)

Before use, RPC must be enabled in aria2 and cross-origin requests from this site must be allowed. After filling in the fields, you can click **Test connection** to verify that the configuration is correct.

Download settings are saved in your local browser only and are not synced to your account.

## Preferences

![The preferences section of the settings](/assets/images/account-settings/en/site@2x.webp)

### Language

Choose the interface language (简体中文, English, 日本語). The page switches to the chosen language immediately, and the emails sent to you also use this language.

### Theme

Choose Light, Dark or System. This setting is saved in your local browser only.

### Adult content

Controls how games and images with adult content are shown. There are three options:

- **Hide** — games and images with adult content are not shown
- **Mask** — games are shown; images are covered by default and can be viewed after you click them
- **Show** — everything is shown directly

Changes take effect immediately. When you are not signed in, games and images with adult content are not shown.

### Other

- **Only games with resources** — the default for the game library and search; it can be changed temporarily on each visit
- **Show the page backgrounds of other users** — when off, the profiles of other users use the default background of the site
